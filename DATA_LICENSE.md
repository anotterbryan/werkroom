## What's in `data/`

The dataset is a compilation of **facts** — names, dates, seasons, placements, challenge
names, song titles, episode titles, and similar competition records. Individual facts are
not copyrightable. The repository contains **no** copyrighted creative material: no logos,
no trade dress, no photographs, no episode footage or screenshots, no song lyrics, and no
social-media handles.

1. Attribute "RPDR Tracking" (and link back to the repository), and
2. Share derivative datasets under the same license.

## Sources

Data is verified against primary, publicly available sources — principally the English
Wikipedia season and episode pages, with Fandom season pages and Paramount+ synopses used
as secondary references. The project's rule is to **never invent data**: unknown values are
left as `-` rather than guessed.
